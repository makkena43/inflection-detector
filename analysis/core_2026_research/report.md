# C2 / C3 / C5: 11 shortlisted 2026 companies

Analysis date: 27 October 2026 (IST). Reconciled 11 distinct names using current rulebook v5.1 and historical entry-week features. This is a qualitative company assessment, not a new validated strategy or a live-price buy instruction.

## Recommendation

Best fundamental research priorities: Dynacons and Ceigall, followed by Krystal. Most interesting conditional catalyst/trading watch: Bluspring. PSP is an execution and margin-recovery watch. Apex is an interesting SME growth business but requires small-risk treatment and contract/cash verification. Rico and Anondita need better entry/earnings economics. NIBE is a high-risk execution watch. I would avoid chasing Sigma or treating Swan as a repeatable low-risk setup. No name earns an unconditional buy from this dataset.

**Fresh-entry limitation:** loaded adjusted NSE prices stop on 9 October 2026, eighteen days before this review. Filings were reviewed through the available 27 October cutoff; Sep-2026 holdings are generally available. Moving averages, valuation snapshots and returns below are dated 9 October, not live quotes. Some newer documents are available from official company sites beyond the local pipeline; these are forward-looking research updates, never retroactively applied to historical selection.

## What the combos actually mean

- C2: order/revenue >=25%, last entry price within 20% of the available 52-week high, six-month pre-entry run-up >60%. This can find strength but also late-stage momentum.
- C3: order/revenue >=25%, operating-margin proxy <10%, at least two of three technical checks. **There is no positive-margin floor**; loss-makers can qualify.
- C5: FII ownership rose QoQ, promoter holding >=60%, near the available 52-week high. It imposes no minimum magnitude of FII buying and no valuation, cash-flow or profitability requirement.

## 1. Dynacons Systems & Solutions Limited (DSSL)

**Assessment:** Priority fundamental candidate; wait for price/earnings confirmation.

**Business and catalyst.** The clearest named-customer commercial catalyst among these picks. RBI awarded private-cloud infrastructure supply, integration and maintenance worth ₹750.82 crore INCLUDING GST over five years. This is not ₹750 crore of immediate revenue: straight-line averaging is about ₹150 crore/year gross, and the actual mix is front-loaded hardware plus ongoing support. FY26 consolidated revenue ₹1,424 crore and PAT ₹84.8 crore provide an existing profitable base. The latest extracted TTM operating-margin proxy is about 11%.

**Quality of earnings / funding.** Audited FY26 consolidated operating cash flow was ₹46.13 crore versus PAT ₹84.81 crore, down from ₹66.04 crore operating cash flow a year earlier. Receivables absorbed ₹165.61 crore. PPE/intangible additions of ₹65.00 crore exceeded operating cash generation, although a CWIP movement partly offset investing flows. Procurement credit, acceptance milestones and customer payment terms matter more than the contract headline. Do not value a hardware-led integrator like a high-margin software company.

**Ownership.** September 2026 promoter holding 60.89%; FII 1.28%, up from 1.08% in June. This supports monitoring, but the 0.20 pp rise is modest.

**What would make it actionable.** Track RBI milestone billing, collection and execution margin; require cash conversion to improve and a fresh price base/trend recovery before treating this as a short-term entry. At the stored 9 October price it was below both 50- and 200-session averages.

**Thesis fails if:** Receivables continue growing faster than sales, rising procurement borrowing, repeated milestone delays, or margin gains without cash.

Evidence: [company/exchange filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/DSSL_04052026185431_INTIMATIONTOSTOCKEXCHANGESRBI.pdf); [supporting filing](https://www.dynacons.com/dynacons-annual-report-2025-26.pdf).

## 2. Ceigall India Limited (CEIGALL)

**Assessment:** Priority infrastructure candidate; conditional accumulation research.

**Business and catalyst.** Established infrastructure execution with a diversified project pipeline. FY26 consolidated revenue ₹4,022 crore and PAT ₹309 crore. The May company call reported ₹11,332 crore order inflow, approximately 4.8x book-to-bill and consolidated debt/equity around 0.6x. This gives visibility, but an order book is an execution obligation, not profit. The original shortlist ₹1,089 crore HAM/project-cost figure is multi-year and should not all be treated as immediate EPC turnover.

**Quality of earnings / funding.** The original consolidated FY26 cash-flow statement reports operating cash outflow ₹91.28 crore, despite positive standalone operating cash flow. HAM and renewable SPVs tie up funding; contracted financial assets and other current assets can absorb cash. Diversification into new developer/renewable verticals brings execution and financing risk. The standalone debt ratio must not be substituted for the consolidated ratio.

**Ownership.** Promoter 82.06% in September, unchanged QoQ; FII declined from 3.88% to 3.46%. The historical C5 signal therefore is not a claim of continuing FII accumulation today.

**What would make it actionable.** Prioritise appointed dates, financial closures, construction progress and completed asset monetisation. Require consolidated cash-flow/funding progress. Stored price was above 50/200-session averages, but fresh quotes are required.

**Thesis fails if:** SPV equity obligations expand without recycling, appointed dates slip materially, or operating cash outflow persists while reported profit grows.

Evidence: [company/exchange filing](https://ceigall.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Earning-Call-07052026.pdf); [supporting filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ceigall_07052026143144_Outcome.pdf).

## 3. Bluspring Enterprises Limited (BLUSPRING)

**Assessment:** Most interesting conditional trading/turnaround watch among the 11.

**Business and catalyst.** A real business-mix change, not just a large-order headline: the company completed STEAG India acquisition on 21 May 2026. Disclosed four contracts total ₹5,112 crore OVER FIVE YEARS; ₹3,487 crore is new contracts and ₹1,625 crore renewals. Simple averaging is ₹1,022 crore/year gross, but a large share replaces an existing STEAG revenue base, not all incremental Bluspring growth. The target company had over ₹700 crore FY26 revenue. Management expects margin accretion; this is guidance, not delivered profit.

**Quality of earnings / funding.** Bluspring FY26 consolidated revenue ₹3,382 crore, PAT loss ₹23.04 crore and operating cash flow ₹51.60 crore. Profitability is still thin, and acquired-company integration plus customer concentration are material. Four major contracts are within the Vedanta/BALCO ecosystem. Avoid adding renewals twice or counting the acquired base as organic growth. Foundit/other subsidiary funding needs can offset service gains.

**Ownership.** September promoter holding 58.41%; FII 5.28%, down from June 5.70%. MF 8.07%. The IPO/demerger price history is short; early technical signals need extra caution.

**What would make it actionable.** Require STEAG consolidation to produce PAT and cash-flow gains, not merely revenue. Monitor parent/subsidiary leverage separately. Price remained above both moving averages in the last stored data; prefer a fresh confirmed base or orderly pullback over chasing an extended candle.

**Thesis fails if:** Revenue jumps without improved consolidated PAT/OCF, substantial acquisition-funded debt/dilution, adverse renewal economics or Vedanta customer payment delays.

Evidence: [company/exchange filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/BLUSPRING_06072026110142_PressRelease.pdf); [supporting filing](https://www.bluspring.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/IP-1.pdf); [supporting filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/BLUSPRING_19052026220825_Outcome.pdf).

**What would make it actionable.** Look for execution-led revenue acceleration, sustained debtor collection and improved cash conversion. At the stored date, price was below its 50-session average but above its 200-session average: mixed, not a fresh confirmed momentum signal.

## 5. PSP Projects Limited (PSPPROJECT)

## Risk controls for the requested quick-gain approach

The earlier take-profit research is historical; a profitable business can still be a poor short-term entry. For new positions, demand fresh quotes and liquidity, identify the next dated execution/earnings catalyst, and write an invalidation and maximum loss before entry. A +50% target is an exit instruction, not a probability estimate. Do not average into an adverse fundamental development solely because the stock still matches an old combo. If no name has both current confirmation and acceptable risk, holding cash is a valid outcome.

No price target or position-size allocation is issued because current quotes, your existing exposure, liquidity needs and maximum tolerable loss are not available. The report identifies research priorities and confirmation conditions without inventing a promised return.

## Data issues to fix in the dashboard

1. Add an explicit positive operating-margin floor when a user wants profitable C3 businesses; preserve a separate turnaround screen for negative-margin names.
2. Display order value including/excluding GST, delivery period, annual execution estimate and new order versus renewal separately. Never sum presentation repeats as new demand.
3. Repair Sigma face-value/share-count normalisation and reconcile all post-merger/preferential allotments before any valuation or return ranking.
4. Treat incomplete SME half-year data separately from four-quarter TTM rather than assuming zero margins.
5. Use actual quarter-end ownership reports, and show dilution/reclassification separately from confirmed market transactions. A missing pledge percentage is unknown, not proof of no pledges.
6. Refresh prices after 9 October before claiming any signal or target is actionable now.
