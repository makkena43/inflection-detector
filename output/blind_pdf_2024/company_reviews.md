## Basis and interpretation

Read the supplied prompt first. Assessments use only each masked PDF and its own cutoff date. No web research, later prices, unmasked packets or company-identity lookups were used. The answer-sheet structure was checked only after all 38 calls had been recorded; its 38 verdict/conviction rows were blank. The supplied answer sheet has been populated separately from this report. This is a historical cutoff-date assessment, not a recommendation at October 2026 prices.

BUY means an entry is justified on the available cutoff-date evidence, subject to the stated valuation limitations. WATCH means a plausible business or catalyst needs more evidence or a better entry. AVOID means the current packet/valuation fails the investment test; it is not an allegation of wrongdoing. Conviction measures confidence in the verdict, not confidence that the stock will rise. `rerating_months` is an approximate review/catalyst horizon, not a predicted return or guaranteed rerating; 0 for AVOID means no positive rerating thesis is underwritten.

For valuation, I generally recompute ratios from the packet's last quoted market cap and financial snapshot. Separate entry-date dashboard values sometimes differ; they are not silently blended with the last-close values. Financial snapshots and ownership tables have no individual evidence IDs; references to them are to the named sections of the same PDF. Bracketed UUIDs cite actual labelled passages. First-reviewer estimates are explicitly distinguished from quoted issuer evidence. Scenario profit calculations apply a stated historical or hypothetical margin to gross revenue and are illustrative; they are not forecasts, do not prove incremental demand, and do not replace contract-specific cash analysis. PAT attributable to owners is preferable to group PAT when minorities are material.

Most packets omit a full current balance sheet, receivables ageing, cash-flow statement, pledge disclosure and detailed payment terms. Absence of a red flag is not proof of no debt, no pledges or clean governance. Historical debt-free statements may be stale. Firm order totals are not automatically revenue in the next four quarters, and capacity commissioning is not an offtake commitment. Consortium shares, GST, renewals, duplicate announcements and O&M tails are treated separately. No uniform 50% next-year execution assumption from the dashboards is accepted without underlying evidence.
