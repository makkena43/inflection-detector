## Scope and method

Applied `/Users/makkenasrinivas/inflection-jobs/expert/prompt_v2.txt` to the same masked PDFs and their original historical cutoff dates. Used no external research, subsequent prices, unmasked company packets or answer labels. The original v1 files and supplied `answers.csv` remain unchanged. The separate 38-row combined CSV incorporates these 21 decisions and carries forward the 17 original BUY/AVOID rows without re-review. Its totals are **14 BUY, 8 WATCH and 16 AVOID**, but those totals must not be described as an all-company v2 test.

V2 explicitly prioritises a firm order of at least 25% of revenue or market cap, or verified twelve-month firm order flow of at least 50% of revenue. These are total order-value comparisons; they are not automatic next-year revenue forecasts. Price strength and relative outperformance receive positive weight. High valuation alone is not an AVOID or an automatic BUY veto. Capacity estimates, gross consortium figures, prospective bids and duplicate announcements still do not become firm incremental demand.

Interpretation of red flags: `none` means no established hard v2 disqualifier, not no risks or a verified clean balance sheet. Documented net promoter selling is a red flag even when small, as the prompt specifies. A large unexplained promoter ownership reduction is also flagged; it is not called proven market selling. Percentage reductions explained substantially by a disclosed equity issue are treated as dilution risks, not automatically as promoter disposal. Minor percentage drift without identified selling is monitored. This distinction matters especially for Companies 03, 16, 21, 33, 35 and 38.

V2 says strong price action supports a BUY, not that it is mandatory. Companies 16, 18, 23 and 29 therefore can qualify on their firm order despite weak or unavailable trend confirmation; their convictions are limited. A stock below a falling 200-day average would be an explicit disqualifier, but none of these 21 has that flag. Missing margins, cash statements or execution terms limit conviction and may make a catalyst unprovable; they are not fabricated evidence of stress.

Conviction follows v2's investment-commitment scale: 5 full-position, 4 strong case with one central unresolved issue, 3 good case with real doubts, 2 weak case, 1 barely a case. It is not confidence that the verdict or price direction will prove right. No 5/5 recommendation is justified here. `rerating_months` is a catalyst/review horizon, not a return forecast; 0 means no positive rerating thesis is underwritten. The historical pattern statements in the prompt are accepted as the requested prior, not independently verified backtest results.

Ratios are labelled as entry-dashboard or last-close where these differ. FY revenue is identified when TTM is unavailable. Ownership, financial and trend tables have no passage IDs; their claims refer to those named sections of each cited PDF. UUIDs reference the exact labelled passages, and reviewer-only values are identified as such. The full v1 report remains the supporting record for unchanged background financial details.
